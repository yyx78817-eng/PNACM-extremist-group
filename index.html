PNACM

Political Science Research & Documentation Repository

The Pro-Nazistic and Anti-Communist Movement (PNACM) is a researched extremist organization aiming to revive National Socialism while opposing communism and geopolitical influences. The movement gained online presence around July 2026, led anonymously by figures designated as PF.395 and PCF.679.

History & Ideology

The organization rejects traditional Western capitalism and Marxist economics, proposing "The Total Synthesis" which merges a state-run economy with racial nationalism and autarky.

Structure & Reception

Command relies on anonymity through alpha-numeric tracking (IM-Codes, AM-Codes, M-Codes) and visual identifiers. The group is widely condemned by democratic institutions and scrutinized by security analysts.
